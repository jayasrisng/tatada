# Five-star usability check

Recruit five travelers with their own upcoming trips. Include one family visit needing only flights, one overnight international flight, one multi-stop trip, and at least one Android and one iPhone. Owner obtains consent; keep recordings and booking documents private. Use no actual purchase, message, check-in or print without a separate explicit approval.

Give each person 15 minutes, without coaching:
1. Open their trip. Import one real confirmation (PDF or pasted text), correct a field, identify unknown values and save. Try the same confirmation again. Find/download/delete the original; locate the separate passport vault without uploading anything there.
2. Tell you what happens next, which local time applies, and where the address/reference lives. For an overnight flight, compare both local dates with their airline confirmation.
3. Download the trip. Close the browser page, enable airplane mode and reopen it. Find the itinerary, address and their own ticket. Explain which information may be stale. Reconnect and confirm previously pending edits remain available.
4. Review a Fo reply as a task or stop, edit it, apply once, reload, and undo. Ask whether Tatada booked or confirmed anything; correct conclusions using provider evidence only.
5. Inspect a share preview. Identify omitted private information, open a link on another device, revoke it and refresh. Export their workspace and find deletion/retention controls without actually deleting their account.

Record task completion without help, corrections, uncertainty, time to find the next step, offline success on a true browser/phone restart, and “Could you put your phone away now?” (1–5). Stop and record the exact failure if an essential detail cannot be retrieved. Do not count assisted completion as uninterrupted success.

Decisions for the owner after observation: whether labeled-field parsing covers their actual confirmation formats; whether times/address/reference need different prominence; how much offline editing travelers expect; whether Fo previews make saved records and confirmed actions clear; acceptable backup retention. Collect five short anonymized notes, not booking codes or document copies, in the feedback summary. Do not invent traveler feedback.
