## Failed attempt and repairs

Attempt 1 (2026-10-19T14-00-21-955Z) stopped after trip creation because research returned no final text. A diagnostic reproduction confirmed the response used its output budget on reasoning and searches. The research endpoint now allows 6,144 output tokens, can resume one paused turn while preserving original provider blocks, shares a 90-second overall budget, and returns a clearer explanation when no answer can be produced. Each request permits at most three searches, with at most two requests per research operation. Response metadata records actual web_search calls without storing thinking blocks, signatures or encrypted search results in the trace.

Attempt 2 (2026-10-19T14-04-20-424Z) completed after two runner interruptions: a transient prepared-message screen was mistaken for approval, and the runner waited for an enabled input while Fo was working. These were runner issues, not evidence of a blocked provider request. The runner was corrected and resumed the existing conversation. End-to-end wall time includes that recovery; Fo reply availability was approximately 91 seconds after the app accepted the send.
